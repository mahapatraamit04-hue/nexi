Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They let the site remember your actions and preferences, keep you signed in, and help the site owner understand how the site is used. Cookies set by the site you are visiting are “first-party” cookies. Cookies set by another company, such as an analytics provider, are “third-party” cookies.

We may also use pixels, tags, local storage, and software development kits. They work in similar ways to cookies, and this policy covers them too.

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies stay on your device until they expire or you delete them. The length depends on the purpose of each cookie, and we set expiry periods no longer than reasonably needed.
